About Bacoor City

History

Bacoor City is one of the oldest and most historically significant cities in Cavite and played a key role in the Philippine Revolution. It has grown from an agricultural town into a highly urbanized gateway city.

Vision

A modern, resilient, and sustainable urban center empowered by technology and transparent governance.

Mission

Deliver efficient and inclusive public services while promoting safe and livable communities.
