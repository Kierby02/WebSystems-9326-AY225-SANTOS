About Bacoor City

History

Bacoor City is one of the oldest and most historically significant cities in Cavite and played a key role in the Philippine Revolution. It has grown from an agricultural town into a highly urbanized gateway city connecting Cavite and Metro Manila.

Vision

A modern, resilient, and sustainable urban center empowered by technology, transparent governance, and an engaged citizenry.

Mission

Deliver efficient and inclusive public services, promote economic growth, and ensure safe and livable communities for all residents.
