About Bacoor City

Learn about our history, vision, and mission for a better tomorrow

Our History

Bacoor City, located in the province of Cavite, Philippines, has a rich and storied past dating back to the Spanish colonial period. The city played a crucial role in the Philippine Revolution, serving as a strategic location for revolutionary forces.

Officially became a city on June 23, 2012, through Republic Act No. 10160, transforming from a first-class municipality into a component city. Today, Bacoor stands as one of the fastest-growing cities in the region, balancing urban development with cultural preservation.

Vision & Mission

Vision: A progressive, sustainable, and resilient Bacoor City where every citizen enjoys quality of life, inclusive growth, and meaningful participation in governance.

Mission: To deliver excellent public service through transparent governance, innovative programs, and collaborative partnerships that empower communities and promote social equity.
