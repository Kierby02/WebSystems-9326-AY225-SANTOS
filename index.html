About Bacoor City

History:
Bacoor City is one of the oldest and most historically significant cities in the Province of Cavite. It played an important role during the Philippine Revolution and is recognized as one of the cradles of independence in the region. From a former agricultural town, Bacoor has developed into a highly urbanized city with growing residential communities, commercial centers, and infrastructure. It also serves as a key gateway connecting Metro Manila and Cavite.

Vision:
Bacoor City envisions becoming a modern, resilient, and sustainable urban center — empowered by technology, guided by transparent governance, and supported by an active and informed citizenry.

Mission:
The City Government of Bacoor is committed to delivering efficient, transparent, and inclusive public services, strengthening community welfare programs, promoting economic growth, and ensuring safe, livable, and environmentally responsible communities for all residents.
